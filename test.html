AquaPredict

A full-stack groundwater intelligence platform built on 22 years of CGWB (Central Ground Water Board) monitoring data from 28,076 wells across 24 Indian states. Combines classical ML and deep learning for spatial and temporal groundwater level prediction.

Data spans quarterly readings from Jan 1996 to Jan 2017. Missing values imputed with site-level medians. Date parsing handles DD/MM/YYYY format.

ML Architecture

Three-stage prediction pipeline:

  • Stage 1 — KNN spatial lookup finds nearest monitoring well to input coordinates
  • Stage 2 — Historical time series extracted for that well
  • Stage 3 — Selected model (LR / RF / LSTM / CNN+LSTM) forecasts future levels

Dataset Statistics

  • 28,076 unique CGWB monitoring wells
  • 24 Indian states covered
  • 83 date columns (quarterly, 1996–2017)
  • Well types: Dug, Bore, Tube, Slim Hole
  • Depth range: 0.5 m – 90+ m below surface
  • 617,000+ yearly well-level records
